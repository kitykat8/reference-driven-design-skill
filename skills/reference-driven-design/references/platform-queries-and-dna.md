# Reference Search Queries & DNA Extraction Templates by Category

Use this reference when executing **Phase 1 (Reference Search)** and **Phase 2 (Visual Deconstruction)** of the `reference-driven-design` skill.

---

## 1. Book Covers & Editorial Publishing (Обложки книг, журналы, верстка)

### Search Queries (`search_web`)
- `site:pinterest.com "[genre/topic]" book cover design typography`
- `site:behance.net "book cover" OR "editorial design" "[topic/mood]"`
- `site:fontsinuse.com "books" "[genre or aesthetic]"`
- `spinemagazine.co book cover design "[genre]"`
- `"Book Cover Archive" OR "Penguin Modern Classics" OR "Verso Books" cover design`

### Key DNA Parameters to Extract
- **Cover Architecture**: Full-bleed art vs. framed box vs. band/tri-part grid (like Penguin/Suhrkamp/Gallimard).
- **Title Lockup**: Placement (top-aligned, optical center, bottom-heavy, vertical), scale relative to trim size, line breaks, and kerning.
- **Author & Subtitle Hierarchy**: Contrast in typeface (e.g., serif title + tracked sans-serif author name), separator elements (hairline rules, fleuron ornaments, brackets).
- **Spine & Back Cover System**: Spine orientation, publisher colophon placement, barcode integration, blurb column width.
- **Tactile Cues**: Paper stock tone (uncoated cream vs. stark white vs. dyed black), foil/emboss accents, spot color tension.

## 2. Logos, Marks & Brand Identity (Логотипы и айдентика)

### Search Queries (`search_web`)
- `site:behance.net "[industry]" "brand identity" OR "visual identity" logo`
- `site:bpando.org "[industry]" brand identity`
- `site:logobook.com "[geometric shape or concept]"`
- `site:pinterest.com "[industry]" logo design minimalist OR brutalist OR editorial`
- `site:fontsinuse.com "branding" "[industry]"`

### Key DNA Parameters to Extract
- **Mark Taxonomy**: Wordmark, monogram/lettermark, abstract geometric symbol, heraldic crest/emblem, or dynamic typographic system.
- **Geometry & Construction**: Stroke weight uniformity vs. calligraphic contrast, negative space cutouts, corner radii (razor-sharp `0px` vs. optical ink-trap rounding), construction grid ratio (e.g., 1:4 stroke-to-counter).
- **Lockup Proportions**: Distance between symbol and wordmark (measured in symbol units or cap-height `X`), horizontal vs. stacked arrangement.
- **Brand Extension Language**: How the logo's geometry echoes into patterns, layout frames, typography, and color blocking on mockups/stationery.

## 3. Presentations & Pitch Decks (Презентации и питч-деки)

### Search Queries (`search_web`)
- `site:behance.net "pitch deck" OR "presentation design" "[industry/style]"`
- `site:pinterest.com "presentation design" OR "keynote layout" editorial grid`
- `site:dribbble.com "pitch deck" "[industry]"`

### Key DNA Parameters to Extract
- **Master Grid & Running Elements**: Running header/footer metadata (section name, slide index `01 / 14`, confidential tag, hairline divider).
- **Slide Rhythm & Archetypes**:
  1. *Cover Slide*: Bold typographic tension or editorial split.
  2. *Statement / Divider Slide*: High-contrast background flip with oversized display typography.
  3. *Data / Metric Slide*: Oversized hero numbers (`120px+`) paired with tight analytical captions and clean chart styling.
  4. *Editorial Split Slide*: Asymmetric 4-column or 12-column grid (e.g., 4 cols sticky thesis on left, 8 cols structured evidence on right).
- **Data Visualization Style**: Line thickness, monochromatic + 1 accent highlight for the key data series, absence of chartjunk/3D effects.

## 4. Posters, Banners & Social Media Visuals (Постеры, баннеры, соцсети)

### Search Queries (`search_web`)
- `site:pinterest.com "[style: swiss / editorial / brutalist / luxury]" poster design`
- `site:behance.net "social media identity" OR "event identity" "[industry]"`
- `site:fontsinuse.com "posters" "[mood]"`

### Key DNA Parameters to Extract
- **Reading Distance Hierarchy**: Level 1 (seen from afar / scroll-stopper), Level 2 (key offer/date/headline), Level 3 (fine-print metadata grid).
- **Framing & Tension**: Bleed edges vs. thick architectural borders, overlapping type on imagery, diagonal vs. orthogonal tension.

## 5. Reference Presentation & Approval Gate Template (Шаблон показа референсов на согласование)

Use this concise structure to present your found references to the user and **wait for their approval** before building the design:

```markdown
### Подобранные референсы (Selected References)
1. **[Название / Студия — Платформа (Behance / Pinterest / Awwwards / Fonts In Use)]** (ссылка)
   - *Композиция и сетка*: ...
   - *Типографика*: ...
   - *Цвет и детали*: ...
2. **[Название / Студия — Платформа]** (ссылка)
   - *Композиция и сетка*: ...
   - *Типографика*: ...
   - *Цвет и детали*: ...

### Как планирую объединить (Предварительная идея синтеза)
- **Из Референса 1**: [например, модульную сетку и пропорции негативного пространства]
- **Из Референса 2**: [например, контраст антиквы и гротеска + тонкие разделительные линии 0.5pt]

---
**Как тебе такие референсы и направление?**
- Если **нравится** — беру их в работу и делаю дизайн.
- Если **не нравится** (или хочется поменять один из пунктов) — скажи, в какую сторону скорректировать, и я найду другие референсы!
```
